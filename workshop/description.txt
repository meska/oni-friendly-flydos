[h1]Friendly Flydos[/h1]
[b]Current version: 1.0.0[/b]

Makes Flydos friendlier to operate while preserving ONI's normal chores, priorities, and drowning behavior.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Automatically selects safe rechargeable and raw-metal power banks on new Flydos.
[*]Keeps atomic power banks excluded by default, with an option to include them.
[*]Gives every Flydo one visible priority shared by battery delivery and emergency rescue.
[*]Adds a Soldering Station controller that maintains a shared minimum number of living Flydos per asteroid.
[*]Coordinates multiple participating stations without reserving materials for the same production deficit.
[*]Shows active Flydos under Industrial Products in the Resources screen without making storage compactors collect them.
[*]Automatically asks a duplicant to carry a submerged living Flydo to the nearest dry reachable cell.
[*]Preserves vanilla drowning behavior if no duplicant completes the rescue in time.
[*]Cleans obsolete rescue markers and safely handles references left by earlier versions.
[*]Checks the authoritative Steam package directly when ONI's legacy Workshop cache is stale.
[/list]

[b]Configuration[/b]
[list]
[*]Automatic power-bank selection can be disabled.
[*]Atomic power banks can be included explicitly.
[*]The default battery-delivery and rescue priority is configurable from 1 to 9.
[*]Each Soldering Station can join or leave automatic production and choose its target Flydo count.
[/list]
